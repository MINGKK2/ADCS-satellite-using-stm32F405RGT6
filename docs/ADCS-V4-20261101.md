# ADCS V4 — deployment and current behavior, 2026-11-01

## Changes since the previous published snapshot

- Common right-handed sensor/body rotation `(legacy Y, -legacy X, legacy Z)`
  applied consistently to gyro, accel, magnetometer and trained Yaw axis.
  Physical Roll remains PA6; Pitch PA7. Euler angles are recomputed from the
  transformed attitude, rather than swapping HTML labels.
- New `axis_frame.h` keeps Flash gyro-bias encoding in the historical body basis
  and rotates at load/save. Existing valid raw-sensor accel/mag calibration stays
  in its original basis. Calibration journal remains backward compatible.
- Explicit VTOR initialization and normal IRQ enable at application entry for
  ROM-GO startup; DMA and USART1 initialized before telemetry. UART remains
  PA9/PA10, DMA2 streams 2/7, 115200 8N1.
- Reversed Pitch control polarity from -1 to +1; Roll +1 / Yaw -1 unchanged.
- Reduced all three Kp values once: 28/2.4 = 11.6666667. Ki2.4/Kd8 unchanged.
  DIAG reports rounded milli-unit values `ATK_u3=11667,2400,8000`.
- Viewer shows fitted encoders correctly: Roll absent, Pitch PC6, Yaw PA0;
  stale/invalid data is marked. Long log records wrap within the control panel.
  Gain help refers to telemetry instead of a fixed outdated Kp label.
- Current C3/S3 firmware is radio-only, with sleep disabled, callback-paced TX,
  bounded USB diagnostics and complete-record reassembly at S3. The canceled
  wireless STM32 updater is not part of the current code.

## Verification

The exact V4 ELF/BIN/HEX/map and hashes are in `artifacts/stm32-v4/`.
STM32 full Debug build passed without warnings/errors. Existing tests exercising
actual PID/EKF/encoder functions, parser/DMA, Flash compatibility, protocol and
viewer passed. The ELF loads through 0x0801DDFC and does not load sector 11.

USB DFU programmed sectors 0–4, verified all image bytes and issued GO. The full
128 KiB sector-11 hash before/after was identical; no raw calibration dump is
published. STM32 subsequently enumerated its application USB COM port.

This publication does not claim motor stability. No agent-driven motor test or
PID-enable was sent after V4. Pitch reaction-torque sign and reduced-gain control
response have not been checked physically. No new gain-identification data was
provided; coefficients are operator-requested values, not a completed tuning.

Radio-only source was built and flashed successfully, and earlier hardware runs
showed about 19 Hz telemetry and real STM32 STATUS ACKs. Subsequent C3 USB-removal
and post-V4 tests had no RF response; C3 supply/connection remains a diagnostic
item. The last post-flash capture contained S3 local diagnostics only. Old viewer
values or a successful DFU verify are not proof of current fresh IMU data.

## Next hardware checks

With motor power off, establish a stable C3 supply/common GND and verify live
`FW=AXIS_FRAME_RP_PITCH_KP_V4`, `ATK_u3=11667,2400,8000`, `ATT_VALID=1`, fresh
`IMU_AGE_ms`, calibration loaded from Flash, and a real `ACK CMD=STATUS` via S3.
Then verify one axis at a time under the actual fixture before enabling combined
control. A one-channel wheel encoder does not determine rotation direction or
body attitude. Retain ESTOP and speed/invalid-feedback protections.

Older documents/artifacts describe their historical snapshots. Current build
and radio behavior is defined by the V4 source and this record.
